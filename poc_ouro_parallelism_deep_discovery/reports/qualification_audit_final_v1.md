# Final qualification audit v1

## Outcome and scope

Zero current finalists is a defensible qualification decision.
It is not a count of measured architecture failures.
It does not follow from absent candidate LIVE_E2E results alone.

No current card combines a distinct recurrence-specific discriminator, a credible execution contract, and supporting incremental useful-cost evidence.
Some cards contain legal contracts or positive component facts.
Those facts do not complete that conjunction.

This is a bounded audit for the final synthesis.
It is not the final campaign verdict or completion record.
The [structured audit](../artifacts/qualification_audit_final_v1.json) preserves all 19 classifications, source hashes, and limits.
No draft, frozen card, earlier report, or source code changes.

## Qualification rule

[Specification Section 21](spec_snapshot.md) permits zero to three finalists.
Its PROMISING labels do not require completed LIVE_E2E before any early survivor.
They require a distinct mechanism, credible execution, and supporting headroom.
A candidate can remain mode-specific or size-specific with an explicit partial label.
Uniform improvement at every operating point is not required.

The current [Tables A/B/E/F](current_tables_abef_overlay_v1.md) keep novelty, state feasibility, numerical integrity, and useful cost separate.
All 19 novelty fields remain NOT_ESTABLISHED.
All 38 candidate E2E and free-running-quality fields remain NOT_RUN.
These labels describe evidence, not mathematical impossibility.

Each card also lacks a non-E2E prerequisite.
Examples include an unspecified useful representation, an unqualified numerical path, or a missing ownership cost difference.
Thus, the zero-finalist decision does not use missing E2E as its sole premise.

## Three non-rescue reopening prerequisites

These are evidence thresholds, not run recommendations.
They authorize no GPU work, observer retry, fixture retry, or serving engine.

1. Identify one changed physical owner or retained-state lifetime that reduces a named charged cost on identical legal ready work.
   Give bytes, residency, waits, and the resource path against an alias-aware generic control.
   A changed mathematical DAG is not required.
2. Supply a complete backend-valid decision or equality certificate with useful actual checkpoint hits before ordinary work finishes.
   Include every check, fallback state, side effect, and a cost below the removed work.
   Rational controls, static CSE, or hidden-only equality are insufficient.
3. For C19, specify full K+V page/base lifetimes that change a real admission boundary or repeated-read path.
   Include bases, indices, appends, decoder work/scratch, independent coding, and adaptive aliases.
   Keep capacity evidence separate from latency evidence.
