# Audit of negative-claim scope

## Result and limits

The current evidence does not prove that useful recurrence-specific ownership is impossible.
Most detailed reports already state this limit.
Some short dispositions and reopening conditions need narrower interpretation.
This audit records that interpretation without changing earlier reports, cards, status records, or raw data.
It selects no finalist and gives no final campaign verdict.

The important narrowing points are:

1. C14 does not need a changed mathematical dependency to reopen a physical-cost question.
2. C04 can reduce exposed communication cost without removing an entire message.
3. C12's linear-summary obstruction does not reject every nonlinear structure or exact fallback design.
4. C16's ready-set equality does not establish equal distributed execution cost.
5. C19's read-volume model does not measure latency.
6. C07's failed fixture qualification does not establish architecture or task-quality failure.

## Evidence categories

| Code | Evidence | Permitted conclusion | Conclusion it does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Direct mechanism prior, with relevant method or code | The identified primitive or protocol is not a new mechanism by itself. | The prior is a ready Ouro implementation, or every ownership combination has no value. |
| B | Measured scoped cost comparison | The tested implementation loses under its stated shapes, timing boundary, and controls. | Every implementation, workload, precision contract, or ownership layout loses. |
| C | Generic graph expressibility or a constructed equivalence | The representation adds no dependency by itself. A full trace match gives conditional physical equality. | An existing runtime implements the same state protocol efficiently, or no other physical optimum exists. |
| D | Missing evidence | The claim remains unestablished or unqualified. | The mechanism fails. |
| E | Analytic, structural, or measured non-cost result | The stated identity, counterexample, capacity inequality, or observed predicate holds within its assumptions. | A measured latency failure or a broader theorem outside those assumptions. |

E is separate because a numerical counterexample is neither a timing result nor merely missing evidence.
A failed bitwise check also differs from measured task-quality loss.

## Focused narrowing and reopening facts

### C01: ready physical-operator workers

The [ownership audit, lines 34–40](operator_ownership_nearest_audit.md#L34-L40) identifies concrete batching, shared pointers, and event-ordered work in NanoFlow.
It also states that those sources do not establish Ouro's cache, norm, gate, or low-C latency behavior.
Thus A supports the ordinary-pooling prior boundary.
It does not establish a tested complete C01 implementation.

The [persistent screen, lines 116–124](persistent_execution_redteam.md#L116-L124) rejects ordinary complete-core on-chip storage under its capacity assumptions.
It explicitly leaves selective retention and streamed execution open.
That E result is not an HBM miss-rate measurement or proof that no selected weight subset can help.

Reopening fact: specify a named retained subset or ownership change with a legal C1/C2/C4/C8 input frontier.
Show its changed live bytes, transfers, and exposed path against same-P TP/DP and a competent generic pooling control.
A generic executor's ability to express the plan does not, by itself, close that physical question.

### C04: layouts and nonlinear consumers

[The layout controls, lines 107–142](layout_and_structured_residual_redteam.md#L107-L142) reject specific local nonlinear evaluations of additive partials.
They do not prove that every rank must materialize a complete vector before every nonlinear boundary.
The same report provides positive feature-sharded and moment-reduction constructions at lines 90 and 146–171.
Its illustrative collective counts are not a communication lower bound or latency estimate.

The ledger's reopening phrase requires a “named removed message.”
That requirement is too narrow as a physical-cost criterion.
A plan could instead reduce bytes, startup frequency, contention, or exposed synchronization time.
It must still retain equivalent information and the declared numerical contract.

Reopening fact: name the exact consumer-compatible representation and the cost term that decreases.
Account for every deferred gather, reduction, norm statistic, layout conversion, and kernel-shape change.
Compare its complete exposed path with valid generic fusion under the same BF16 policy.
No claim of profit follows from the present positive algebra alone.

### C12: summaries, rank, and exact fallback

[The fixed-feature proof](attention_summary_exactness_screen.md#L19-L45) concerns arbitrary real query/value families through m fixed linear features.
Its rank construction rejects the stated universal claim when m<N.
It does not reject nonlinear indices, restricted-domain representations, or all approximate methods.
The report states those limits explicitly.

The [36/36 cross-depth certificates per model](cross_depth_pattern_certificate.md) concern selected actual key tensors and exact-real score patterns.
They capture no query distribution or values.
They do not prove that all finite-precision patterns or weighted outputs differ.
They also do not prove failure of every history representation.

[C12's line 102](../candidates/C12.md#L102) says to reject if exactness requires full KV.
That phrase needs a narrower target.
Retaining full authoritative KV as fallback can remove a capacity claim without disproving a cheaper exact read or decision procedure.
Its full capacity and fallback cost must remain charged.

Reopening fact: supply a concrete exact representation, or an explicit permitted approximation class, with a current-query algorithm.
State summary size, update cost, fallback frequency/cost, all required KV, numerical limits, and original-gate/commit behavior.
The physical saving must exceed those costs beyond generic attention controls.
The present D result supplies no qualifying design and no recommendation to implement one.

### C14: same DAG, physical trace, and a possible placement optimum

The [reopening audit, lines 120–130](c14_cyclic_prefill_reopening_audit_v1.md#L120-L130) is stronger than graph expressibility alone.

That is a sound conditional equivalence, not a measured stock-runtime result.
The report explicitly says the inspected generic primitives are not a tested Ouro server.

The ledger's reopening condition requires a changed dependency or state mechanism.
That excludes too much.
A different measured placement, retention, or transient-buffer optimum can matter with the same mathematical DAG.
The [alias caution](alias_equivalence_interpretation.md) already permits that case.

The current balanced physical-PP versus TP4 accounting finds equal aggregate dense-core and final retained-KV capacity.
That narrow structural result remains valid.
It does not settle transient buffers, selective retention, boundary representations, or latency.
Known causal prompt chunks also supply prefill concurrency at C1.
The non-speculative one-current-token decode bound must not remove that prefill frontier.

Reopening fact: identify a physical cost difference absent from the granted identical-trace construction.
It can concern capacity-feasible scratch lifetimes, selected retention, or communication/computation overlap.
Compare the resulting causal prefill schedule with tied-aware cyclic PP and native TP under the same capacity and numerical policy.
A generic cyclic-PP win would not automatically become a new execution axis.
A different mathematical dependency is not a prerequisite for investigating that physical result.

### C16: equal ready rows do not imply equal physical cost

[The frontier screen, lines 114–140](mixed_depth_frontier_redteam.md#L114-L140) establishes no extra union width beyond generic mixed-depth batching.
The CPU controls use explicit snapshot-readiness assumptions.
They do not execute a complete resource-constrained model schedule, as lines 224–234 state.
That C/E result rejects the ready-width claim, not every cross-owner cost advantage.

[The card's line 146](../candidates/C16_mixed_depth_frontier.md#L146) says no incremental benefit survives the controls.
Read that statement as “these controls establish no distinct ready-width mechanism.”
No same-resource physical comparison proves zero incremental latency benefit.

An identical ready trace is useful for an isolated service comparison.
In a full run, prior ownership and scheduling choices also change future completion and gate-availability times.
A fixed trace cannot silently substitute for that feedback.

Reopening fact: show a changed cost on the identical ready work, then preserve self-consistent future readiness under common arrivals and semantics.
Charge routing, queue delay, batching delay, idle devices, and authoritative KV/gate publication.
Keep full-KV union, same-P TP/DP, and generic disaggregation as controls.
The eight constructed frontier cases contain no measurements of that advantage.

### C17: numerical obstruction and local versus recurrent value

[FlashNorm and the CPU control](candidate_round3_independent_axes.md#L93-L119) establish two different facts.
Deferred normalization has direct prior overlap.
The simple rewrite also fails universal BF16 equality on a constructed input.
Neither fact measures model quality or latency.

The [tile audit, lines 93–117](tile_dataflow_prior_audit.md#L93-L117) correctly leaves local fusion headroom unmeasured.
It also leaves selective retention open.
An R1 gain identifies generic operator value, but does not numerically predict the complete R4 execution.
R4 can change amortization and contention without changing the local identity.
That possibility alone is not a distinct architecture.

Reopening fact: specify a backend-qualified numerical path and a named retained-byte, setup, or cross-owner effect beyond ordinary fusion.
Charge tile publication, scratch, reductions, occupancy, and synchronization over the actual revisit interval.
Measure incremental benefit against the same permitted generic transformation.
The existing cast counterexample must not become a ban on every qualified reordered implementation.

### C19: encoded size, modeled reads, and latency

[The retained screen](lossless_kv_measurement_screen.md#L24-L43) measures exact encoded sizes for selected keys from one request per model.
It models uncached separate reconstruction across four sequential depths.
It contains no values, GPU codec, HBM counter, or attention timing.
Its common-base modeled read ratios are 1.24077× and 1.15882× of raw captured key bytes.
Independent-coded capacities are 0.71707× and 0.68256×, with the same raw denominator.

The sentence at line 65 says “no latency headroom” under that model.
The defensible conclusion is narrower: **no read-volume advantage under the stated uncached reconstruction model**.
Latency remains unmeasured.
Cache capacity does not prove universal eviction, and a legal retained base cannot receive free-residency credit.
Both limits already appear at lines 38–51.

Reopening fact: identify a base/page ownership and retention lifetime that changes the modeled repeated reads without assuming simultaneous future queries.
Charge complete K+V, metadata, decoder work, appends, scratch, and real retained capacity.
Compare independent coding and adaptive aliases first.
A capacity-only claim also needs a named admission boundary within its stated workload, not merely fewer encoded bytes.
The current native TP baseline has no remote historical-KV transfer to remove.
