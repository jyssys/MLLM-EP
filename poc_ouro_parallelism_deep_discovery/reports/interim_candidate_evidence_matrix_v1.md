## C07 and C14 interpretation boundaries

The [C07 eager fixture contract](c07_one_pass_fixture_contract_v1.md) measures one ready common-core pass, not closed-loop speculative decoding.
TP4 selects four distinct requests at logical depths 0–3.
TP1 measures one full-weight owner and one row.
Four dependent passes of one token are never treated as four ready inputs.
No measured four-owner makespan can come from combining separate TP1 clocks.
GPU metadata support, parity, memory feasibility, and service time remain pending at this snapshot.
Even a future eager component pass would not establish the strongest compiled-native comparison or task quality.

The [C14 reopening audit](c14_cyclic_prefill_reopening_audit_v1.md) strengthens the previous generic comparison.
Its control matches physical storage and ownership, not only DAG expression.
The installed default pipeline IR duplicates active tied weights.
Correcting that generic default alone cannot establish novelty.
A matched tied-aware cyclic baseline can use the same aliases, KV owners, chunks, kernels, and schedule.
This control does not rule out every measured cyclic-PP advantage over TP4.
The earlier single-current-token decode concurrency bound must not reject known causal prefill chunks.

## Storage and binding caveats

Storage controls are generic counterfactuals, not an additional candidate architecture.
Their controlled E2E measurements do not populate candidate E2E or quality fields.

The [v1/v2 validity warnings](full_core_storage_results.md) remain in force.
Residency attribution is invalid or unvalidated for those runs.
No old alias/copies timing ratio becomes a causal HBM-sharing result here.

The [fully isolated v4 controls](full_core_storage_v4_checkpoint.md) complete both sizes at P2/P4.
All eight jobs pass saved-graph and cache-root checks.
All four model/TP comparisons still differ numerically.
A fresh same-alias Base1.4B P2 process also differs numerically.
That repeat prevents attributing every numerical difference to the alias/copies intervention.
It does not identify the unique cause.

The [binding audit](storage_binding_and_lowering_audit_v1.md) checks two separate Base1.4B P2 prefill jobs at N1024.
It checks 1928 lifted weight operands and 11568 metadata-field comparisons with zero mismatches.
Those calls use graph mode `NONE`.
They are not the timed v4 cached-decode processes.
The audit does not prove downstream kernel addresses, inspect weight values, or resolve numerical parity.

Equal allocation, interface counts, normalized source, and binding metadata establish different facts.
None independently establishes native HBM traffic, cache-hit rate, or an isolated sharing-dependent latency effect.
The [cache-capacity screen](interpass_cache_capacity_screen.md) is an analytic repeated-stream bound, not such a measurement.
It does not reject every ownership optimum.

## Baseline evidence is not candidate evidence

The [native TP/DP4 cohort analysis](native_tp_dp_cohort_analysis.md) contains completed fixed-R4 generic controls for both sizes.
Its five controlled repetitions per cell are calibration repeats, not independent natural populations.
TP API wall and DP parent-release wall have different boundaries.
The DP residual includes controller, transport, release skew, and worker glue, not pure IPC.
These controls strengthen future comparisons without establishing a candidate gain or original-gate factorial result.

The [state-transform audit](state_transform_frontier_audit_v1.md) adds no new card.
It retains zero qualifying new transforms after the bounded source and algebra checks.
That scoped obstruction does not become a universal impossibility result or a campaign completion claim.
