## Source corrections that must remain

The [primary-claim re-audit](primary_claim_reaudit_v1.md) supersedes broader inherited descriptions.

- CDB computes argmax tokens and feeds them back under controlled lengths and recorded exits. It does not simply replay token values.
- CDB also reports Ouro2.6 recurrent-step latency. That is not its complete adaptive serving matrix.
- SNLP includes post-hoc variants. Its selected verified-drafter E2E speedups are theoretical.
- KernelFlume includes real A6000 batch2–64 scaling. That evidence does not qualify this campaign's Ouro H100 matrix.
- FlashLoop's BF16 switch removes quantization, not its sparsity approximations. Its efficiency baseline disables its own approximations.
- Enabling Dense-Ragged and ETE-Ragged use different prefill policies. ETE does not establish matching support across both Base sizes.

The [RLT scope overlay](public_adaptive_runtime_scope_overlay_v1.md) removes any exclusive-public-code reading of the CDB baseline. RLT adds public immediate-gate and per-row-depth-KV context for C01/C03/C13/C16. Its inspected distributed path uses full-model P/D replicas, not TP2/TP4. WaveFront/RLT gate cast order also differs from pinned CDB. No source-only numerical parity follows.

## Outstanding source gaps

Table E should retain [LoopAhead](loopahead_source_audit.md) as additional C07 lifecycle and scheduling overlap. Its trained LAD, fixed-R4 tests and independent A40 shards do not qualify original-adaptive distributed verification.

CacheFlow's full method and implementation remain unaudited in the retained C10 evidence. Performer and Short-Dot metadata support their broad primitive claims, not target-specific numerical or experimental guarantees. C18's CSE URL is unpinned and only supports static duplicate removal. An identical deployed dynamic coalescer is not established.

Inherited bounded searches did not locate official code for Enabling, LoopAhead or KernelFlume. This limits reproducibility, not mechanism priority. The recurrent sampler's final camera-ready body remains unaudited. Kitsune's later journal body was unavailable. Claims remain pinned to the versions actually inspected.

No change to a candidate disposition follows from this audit. No new GPU work, engine, model run, protected-result access, or modification of old evidence occurred. The ledger records the actual UTC segment. Concurrent worker time does not add campaign elapsed hours.
