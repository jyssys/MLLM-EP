## Evidence update 1

The CPU screen inspects 108 bounded activation pairs per checkpoint.
It reconstructs original BF16 bits exactly through XOR and a reversible byte layout.
Observed size ratios are not GPU codec timings.
Fixed-decode XOR/byte-plane/zlib ratios are approximately 0.779 and 0.771 of the original bytes for the two models.
Raw zlib alone is approximately 0.809 for both.
The observation is a limited input sample, not a universal entropy bound.

The P2 physical probe measures transport startup and an unfused reversible transform separately.
The transform excludes entropy encoding, decoding, metadata, and receiver-base management.
Its overhead is already comparable to a small-message broadcast in this tested path.
No executable compressed serving path or E2E gain is established.

Current status: `NO_NET_HEADROOM` for the tested unfused small-message premise.
A fused codec remains untested and faces generic lossless-transport prior art.
The representation does not expose independent future recurrent work.
