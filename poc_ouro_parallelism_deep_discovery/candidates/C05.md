# C05: Finite-trajectory parallel correction

## 1. Identity

Revision 1. Search family: E. Initial category: potential new execution axis.

## 2. Mechanism

Evaluate versioned recurrent trajectory guesses concurrently and charge all correction and KV repair work.

## 3. Relevant model property

The audited Base checkpoints share physical weights across four passes but retain depth-indexed state.
See `reports/ouro_semantics_audit.md` for pinned source locations.

## 4. Target scope

Both Base sizes, fixed R4, and the unchanged adaptive gate are scope targets, not demonstrated gains.
Primary C is 1/2/4/8. Contexts are 1K/4K/8K. P2/P4 require physical tests. P8 remains projected.

## 5. Baseline and saved cost

Compare mode-matched native 1GPU and the strongest correct same-P conventional baseline.
The hypothesized saving is: A smaller charged number of correction rounds could shorten recurrence span.

## 6. Operation sequence

Four exact sequential passes -> causal initialization -> parallel residual/derivative rounds -> validation -> KV commit.

## 7. Legal DAG and ownership

Each task must identify request, token/chunk, recurrent depth, physical layer, layout, and version.
Weights are immutable. Historical KV belongs to its matching committed request/depth/layer prefix.
Current KV remains private until its complete input and commit rule pass.
The generic validator is `scripts/verify_dag.py`. This card is not a completed candidate-specific executable DAG.

## 8. Source of concurrency

Map evaluations at different depths use guessed states. They are solver tasks, not exact ready successors.

## 9. Exactness and quality

Approximate until a sufficient exact or quality-controlled correction/commit criterion passes.
Quality remains NOT_RUN for a new mechanism.
Use the frozen free-running calibration and held-out protocol before a quality-preservation claim.

## 10. State and memory

Charge unique and replicated weights, full required depth KV, current activations, graph buffers, and candidate scratch separately.
For H=2048, full BF16 KV is 786432 bytes/token at L24/R4 and 1572864 at L48/R4.
These are structural capacities, not measured HBM traffic.

## 11. Messages and topology

Only physical 4/5/6/7 are eligible for P2/P4.
Every message needs a producer, consumer, byte count, and completion dependency.
No zero-cost KV movement or unsupported eight-GPU result is permitted.

## 12. Added costs and critical path

Jacobian or JVP work, reductions, solver vectors, fresh scratch KV, validation, and full internal KV repair.
Use a resource-constrained path. Do not add overlapping intervals or replace serial work with a maximum without ready inputs.

## Evidence update 1

The changed assumption is provisional correction, not authoritative stale-state replacement.
Only a causal embedding or one exact current-token pass initializes the guesses.
The numerical probe evaluates actual pretrained full cores with the correct depth-indexed history.
It overwrites private current-token rows, never future-token history.
Reference future states enter diagnostics only.

Both Base sizes run two single-token probes, one per task domain.
Jacobi and identity-surrogate correction recover the exact boundary trace after four zero-warm-up rounds.
With one exact warm-up pass, three correction rounds recover it.
Current-token KV also matches at those recovery points.
Earlier token agreement can coexist with large internal KV error.

Four ideal core rounds do not beat four sequential core passes before added costs.
The actual numerical executor is serial on one GPU.
It measures no four-way solver speedup or free-running candidate quality.
See `outputs/finite_correction_base14_v1/` and `outputs/finite_correction_base26_v1/`.

Jacobi finite-chain correction, surrogate correction, and KV repair have direct prior art.
See [the parallel sampler audit](../reports/parallel_sampling_nearest_audit.md).
Current status: `NO_NET_HEADROOM` for the tested exact-recovery schedule.
Finite-round approximation remains `QUALITY_UNRESOLVED`, not a qualifying new architecture.
