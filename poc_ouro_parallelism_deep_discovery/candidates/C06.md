## Evidence update 1

The exact real-arithmetic identity requires both `W*base` and the fresh dense `W*delta`.
The second multiplication does not become cheap because delta has a small norm.
Normalization needs a fresh denominator.
SwiGLU and attention softmax cannot generally reuse separately transformed additive components.

The CPU audit includes valid algebra and BF16 counterexamples.
It proves no model-quality property.
The captured BF16 inputs also show few bitwise-equal elements between tested recurrent versions.
This observation does not prove every possible residual representation is dense.
It supplies no exact sparse-compute guarantee.
