# Final decision

## CHARACTERIZATION-SIGNAL

Dormant-Live MASK tokens are real and numerous, but they are not dormant in
the routed-MoE state space.  The primary hypothesis fails at **temporal
stability, full-trajectory quality, deployable selection, and economic
mapping**, not at population discovery.

### Answer to the research question

There is no evidence for a paper-level dormant routed-expert refresh method on
this substrate.  “Future acceptance distance” captures decoder scheduling,
not computational quiescence.  Normal top-8 route/output values keep changing
even for tokens that will not be accepted soon.  Expert-aware invalidation
correctly detects that volatility, but then almost no expensive cacheable work
remains.

No production implementation, custom kernel, Kimi transfer, or expanded
benchmark was justified.  The negative result is still useful: it closes the
tempting ACTIVE/DORMANT/DEAD three-tier abstraction unless a future model
exposes both a larger post-Epoch ceiling and genuinely stable dormant routed
outputs.
