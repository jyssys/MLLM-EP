# Epoch residual analysis

The accounting partitions physical rows without double counting:

- DEAD/STABLE: Epoch territory, 28.95--29.00% routed E2E in this dense-block
  substrate.
- DORMANT-LIVE H=1: candidate territory, 8.09--8.61% optimistic fresh-router
  dispatch/expert/combine mass.
- ACTIVE-LIVE: must remain fresh, 10.85--11.98% routed E2E.

After retaining DeepEP fixed cost, the impossible perfect H=1 post-Epoch
residual is 6.95%/7.27%.  Periodic K8 is 4.98%/5.15%.  Requiring exact route and
destination stability reduces it to 0.63%/0.54%.  A future-free high-precision
proxy reduces it to 2.37%/1.30% at best.

Thus the robust framing is a negative systems fact:

> Epoch can remove dead work, but the remaining still-MASK rows are not a
> quiescent cache tier.  Tokens far from acceptance remain representation- and
> route-active, and their safely deferrable routed-EP cost is small.

This is independent from claiming or measuring Epoch's speedup; Epoch was not
available as an official executable baseline.  It is a source-derived residual
accounting sensitivity.
