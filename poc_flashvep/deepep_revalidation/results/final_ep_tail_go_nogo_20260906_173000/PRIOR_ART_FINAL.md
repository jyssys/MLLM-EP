# Prior-art and novelty note

Because the direct economic and feasible-oracle gates failed, no positive
novelty claim is made. The observation that asynchronous MoE communication can
wait on a dependency is already part of DeepEP/vLLM execution semantics; the
earlier reports also distinguish it from generic communication/computation
overlap and load-imbalance work. A future claim about history-dependent
communication debt would require current-runtime, cross-backend evidence plus
a >=12% direct E2E method result, neither of which is present here.
