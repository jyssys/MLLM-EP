# Mechanism PoCs

`previous async DeepEP communication` -> `notify/barrier or previous-event
dependency` -> `next dispatch span inflates` -> `expert/combine remain ordinary`.

The prior global synchronize reduced some extreme-tail frequency but did not
remove work and was not stable across repeated runs. Relevant communication
stream drain and previous-dispatch threshold policies incurred p50/
throughput cost and failed the selective-policy gate. No new production
mechanism was implemented in this final economic validation.
