## Measurement obligations carried forward

- Request completion/TTFT/ITL/TPOT and fixed SLO goodput, not layer or wave proxies.
- Output correctness gate before accepting performance evidence.
- Same prompt/output/arrival traces; record offered concurrency separately from scheduled batch size.
- Rank-local CUDA events and logical invocation deduplication; no cross-device timestamp subtraction.
- Instrumentation/no-hook control, warmup and paired independent engine restarts.
- Unknown oracle headroom is not zero. Unsupported original scale/model is not method failure.
- Cached old traces may select workloads and support a labelled diagnostic; fresh claims require fresh runs.
