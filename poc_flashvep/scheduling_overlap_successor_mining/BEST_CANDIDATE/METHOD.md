# Successor mechanism: NOT IMPLEMENTED

Only baseline compatibility/measurement adapters were implemented. No large
scheduler or dynamic selector is justified by the finite-space measured regret.

Three **contingent, unvalidated principles** would be ownership-aware stage-cost
partitioning; joint admission/chunk/partition selection under actual token SLOs;
and dependency-aware microbatch execution rather than independent stage minima.
FastPP itself, VPP and adjacent pipeline systems immediately challenge their
novelty. Merely listing them does not establish a research space or headroom.

Cheapest continuation is baseline work: semantics-verified native VL request
identity followed by a small measured joint chunk/partition counterfactual on
one frozen arrival trace. Stop if existing settings recover the loss or added
direct E2E stays below 5%. Do not start with Kimi or a new controller.
