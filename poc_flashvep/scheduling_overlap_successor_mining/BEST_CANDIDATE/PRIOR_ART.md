# FastPP successor novelty disposition

FastPP already models attention context and online correction; adding context
to token cost is not new. Its ALP and rebalancing contest generic chunk changes.

[VPP](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.26523v1) is the closest additional layout/chunk
adversary inspected: virtual folded stages and cross-request packing are already
proposed. Different hardware/output lengths alone are not a new causal problem.
The common matrix compares DynaFlow, TokenWeave, Bullet and FinDEP more broadly.

No distinct material MoE/MLLM failure survives here, so no exact novelty gap is
established. See `../PRIOR_ART_MATRIX.md` and `../FASTPP/PRIOR_ART.md` for primary
references and source-specific limits. No first-observation claim is made.
