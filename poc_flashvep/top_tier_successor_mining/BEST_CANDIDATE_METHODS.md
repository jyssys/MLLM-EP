# SERE — method-space attack, no successor implemented

Three distinct hypothetical principles were considered only to test whether the
remaining limitation opens a research space. They are **not proposed contributions**.

1. Request-local semantic guarantees instead of a shared batch-primary contract.
   This can preserve a target's substitution behavior,but may increase the expert
   working set and remove SERE's original memory advantage. The existing S/rho
   controls must be beaten at matched quality/speed first.
2. Content-conditioned contribution/error certification instead of an offline
   global expert-affinity order. Online certification costs and ACE/AnyExperts/
   expert-substitution prior art are immediate adversaries. No measured residual
   after S4 currently justifies this complexity.
3. Exact backup execution with bounded acceptance/rejection instead of accepting
   approximate substitution unconditionally. Verification/recomputation may
   consume the entire advantage; speculative-MoE work is adjacent and no direct
   overlap oracle has been established here.

All three stop before implementation. The actual comparators are Vanilla,
official SERE setting,and **baseline+existing trivial fix**. There is deliberately
no column relabeling S4,the metadata cache correction,or prefill-only gating as
"our successor". At most incremental deployment guidance is supported.
