# Encoder GPU memory in dense MLLM EPD

The campaign starts from the EADA online failure at `07892cbe7193366ec422f1df82006c6e69f91cec`. It asks whether retaining an exact cached representation on E can help at request boundaries. D keeps its active KV local and runs the original decoder. The three H200 stages keep their original model modules.

## What the experiments establish

- The native E worker has a real memory advantage. It carries 1.260 GiB of vision weights and no language KV. P/D each carry 14.185 GiB of LM weights, 1.260 GiB of vision weights and 42 GiB of KV in the native configuration. E completed 100/100 requests in every touched-arena arm up to 80 GiB. The largest residency-only run left 18.232 GiB CUDA free immediately after touching. An active peer tier allocated 63.998 GiB and completed 188/188 requests, with 40.478 GiB free at drain. Latency-neutral capacity needs its own qualification. [Residency and device memory](./mllm_epd_memory_asymmetry_motivation.md)
- Representation size and reuse scope differ materially. BF16 embeddings cost 7,168 B per visual row and Full28 BF16 KV costs 57,344 B per prompt row. On four real Qwen requests an image-complete prefix was 8.01–8.25× larger than its LM-ready embedding. Potential saved GPU service per GiB favored embeddings by 4.85–5.83×. An exact prefix hit, when eligible, can save more total work. [Representation measurements](./mllm_epd_representation_cache_economics.md)
- The exact peer data path is functional. A native single-request witness loaded 4,608 prefix rows from E into P and matched all 28 layers byte-for-byte on both P and D for that reused region. NIXL carried 4,713 exact P→D rows in each arm. MRoPE and all 16 greedy token IDs matched. On the active 188-request stress trace the 64 GiB peer tier restored 1,018,624 rows across 94 requests with 54.400 GiB of physical read traffic. [Full28 receipt](../mllm_epd_memory_asymmetry_discovery/results/peer_native_full28_correctness.json) · [Online counters](../mllm_epd_memory_asymmetry_discovery/results/online_working_set_pilot.json)
- Reusing the native caches gives a strong starting point. In the Natural149 N1 pilot, scoped E embeddings plus P/D APC cut first-sequence TTFT p95 17.656→11.653 s and raised goodput 0.898→1.202 req/s. The repeated sequence changed 17.901→2.839 s and 0.894→2.523 req/s. Decode tail increases exceeded the 5% guard in both comparisons. In the controlled working-set test, local P72 restored the same 94 prefixes as P8 + E64 and delivered 1.244 versus 1.179 req/s goodput, with TTFT p95 18.864 versus 20.746 s. The peer tier moved data much faster than host DRAM, while still missing the primary online benefit target. [Online results and gates](./mllm_epd_multilevel_cache_online_serving.md)

- Matched embedding placement showed a cold-fill effect in three completely fresh pairs. E VRAM lowered mean paired TTFT p95 by 18.9% and E2E p95 by 19.4% relative to the optimized pinned-host staging bridge. Cold TPOT p95 had mean ratio 1.035 with an upper paired bound of 1.103. Warm goodput gained 1.2%. Neither the decode protection requirement nor a steady-state placement gain was established. [Matched placement results](./mllm_epd_multilevel_cache_online_serving.md#three-independent-placement-pairs)

## Answers to the research questions

| Question | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 1. Latency-neutral E capacity | 80 GiB of touched residency completed native Main100. 64 GiB of active peer retention completed stress188. One fixed-order residency start and changed online tails leave performance neutrality unqualified. |
| 2. Embedding and KV sizes | Qwen R3 3.568–196.875 MiB and image-complete R4 29.422–1,576.422 MiB on four physical cases. Natural149 medians were 86.133 MiB R3 and 692.781 MiB logical full prompt KV. |
| 3. Saved work per GiB | R3 offered 4.85–5.83× the potential service savings per GiB of R4 in the four physical cases. R4's projection assumes an eligible E bypass and includes P compute. |
| 4. Image and document reuse | Original-order Main100 had 123/1,251 prior-image occurrences and 6/100 exact-prefix opportunities. Main400 had 2,263/5,004 and 90/400. The media-available Natural149 subset had 1,511/2,639 and 55/149 respectively. |
| 5. Actual embedding versus prefix hits | First Natural149 combined run observed 1,509 embedding hits, 54 P requests with cached rows, and 896,000 reused P rows. The repeated run observed 2,639 image hits and 1,031,168 P cached rows. The denominators are image occurrences and requests, respectively. |
| 6. Strength of existing caches | Native APC and the scoped encoder cache capture most of the eligible first-trace reuse in this data. The NativeStrong comparison includes inline transport and no retention. Equal-byte placement comparisons hold the stable bridge constant. |
| 7. E versus P/D/host | Active peer stores/loads totaled 0.675 s CUDA-event time versus 2.330 s pinned-host time for equal bytes. The equal-budget local P arm gave higher goodput. Active D placement was held constant. |
| 8. Pending KV | Native P-ready→D-submit p95 stayed below 0.45 ms and native P occupancy samples stayed around 6% at the natural operating point. D EngineCore waiting peaked at one for NativeStrong across the three Natural149 loads. No pending-pool deployment was justified at this operating point. |
| 9. Preempted KV | Both full NativeStrong Natural149 sequences yielded zero preemptions on P and D. Physical swap copies were measured. The normal-workload resume path remained untriggered. |
| 10. Embedding-only versus KV-only | At tight capacity and a repeated-image working set, measured density and same-byte offline replay favor embeddings. Whole-prefix recurrence with enough capacity favors KV for avoided P work. Their online trade-off depends on which stage queues. |
| 11. Multi-level versus strongest single cache | Static and causal hybrid policies were screened at equal bytes. The production-compatible prototype uses budgeted E embeddings with optional exact KV offload. Mixed placement has no established joint-gate advantage over the strongest local control. |
| 12. Workload-dependent allocation | The Natural149 isolated-cost replay favors embeddings at 4 GiB. At 16 GiB the strongest static 25:75 placement retains 1,511 image occurrences and 29 prefix hits. The same prefix working set needs roughly 55 GiB to survive stress188's full cyclic eviction. |
| 13. Actual online benefit | The cache combination improves TTFT and goodput over no retention in the Natural149 pilot. The relative TPOT and ITL gates fail. The exact peer tier improves reuse over default P42 in a controlled trace, but local P72 delivered higher goodput. |
| 14. Transfer and latency protection | Real-copy contention changed E and P enclosures even without remote LM compute. Peer transport improves over host DMA on the matched stress cohort. The measured overall serving pairs do not qualify unconditional SLO protection. |
| 15. Text-only PD | Text-only Qwen native 2P/1D restored 343,040 external peer rows. Equal-budget local24 and peer16/P8 delivered 0.811 s and 0.819 s TTFT p95, with 128/128 joint SLO in both. Local42 gave 0.793 s and 1P/2D local42 gave 0.967 s in this controlled trace. |
| 16. MLLM-specific mechanism | A vision representation has smaller storage and broader potential scope than causal KV. The physical distinction is reproducible. Existing systems cover the key representation/reuse mechanisms, and the campaign has no qualified extra serving benefit over the strongest generic control. |
| 17. Second dense architecture | InternVL3-8B reproduced the roughly 8× payload ratio. Recorded native A/A was 4/4 and supported Embedding injection was exact for 3/3. One stock ragged geometry rejected injected input. A different-question APC pair recovered 6,656 rows with 16/16 token agreement. Second-family online EPD remains `NOT_RUN`. |
| 18. Independent contribution | E VRAM is a workable exact tier and modality changes cache economics. Strong placement controls and prior work make this a useful negative systems result at the tested operating points. |

## Verdicts

**`NO_GO_ONLINE_BENEFIT` for the tested passive peer and multi-level serving paths.** The strongest generic baseline is an existing cache path with enough local P capacity for the working set. At the tested online grid, peer storage improves bytes moved per unit time. It does not deliver the required TTFT, E2E or goodput change together with decode protection and quality. The peer data plane itself is exact on the stored state.

| Direction | Verdict | Decision boundary |
|---|---|---|
| A. Pending KV pool | `NO_GO_MEMORY_PRESSURE` | Natural NativeStrong has low sampled active P KV and sparse D waiting. P queueing is real. The observed data do not establish completed P KV as its source. |
| B. Embedding cache | `NO_GO_NOVELTY` | Actual E cache reuse is valuable in combination with APC. Encoder-only caching is known and the isolated GPU-only arm trails NativeStrong at 2.5 req/s. Differences between E VRAM and pinned host are limited to the measured storage path. |
| C. Prefix KV pool | `NO_GO_ONLINE_BENEFIT` | Exact remote hits evaluated online with six budget placements. At equal total budget the fastest local APC arm remains the latency reference. The best peer goodput moves by about 1% with large decode-tail degradation. |
| D. Preempted KV pool | `NO_GO_MEMORY_PRESSURE` | Actual native P/D preemption counts stay at zero at three offered loads. No active resume arm is justified by these traces. |
| E. Weight cache | `INCONCLUSIVE` | Actual BF16 copy is faster from E scratch. A native model switch and multi-model contention remain unmeasured. |
| Multi-level admission | `NO_GO_ONLINE_BENEFIT` | Static physical hierarchy and fixed-budget controls were exercised. Scope-aware utility adds negligible modeled work reduction over generic. No learned or online joint allocator was deployed. |

A decision beyond this test grid would need an observed cache-pressure regime that the strongest feasible local APC cannot absorb, an exact matched CPU/GPU placement control, and the same joint latency and quality gates. Benchmarks on larger checkpoints or with several models would be new evidence. The source/weight revisions, original media and dataset hashes, exact arrivals, raw trace locations and executed/unexecuted boundaries are kept with the reproductions. ## Evidence boundaries and closeout

The new Qwen-VL serving tables contain 5,134 completed client requests across the candidate, comparison and placement cohorts. The independent 600-request residency screen and 640-request LLM PD control are separate from that count. The model check uses original BF16 weights and actual H200 transport. Raw per-request outputs, stage timelines, environment snapshots and frozen worker overlays remain in the indexed external run directories. The official LongDoc extraction, maximum sustainable QPS, maximum D concurrency, active preemption recovery, GPU weight switching, direct E→D prefix injection, and a second-architecture online run remain `NOT_RUN`. None enters the verdict as positive evidence.

The GPU experiment lease ended before Git publication. All eight original approved `train_lm.py` burner roles were verified, the pause marker was absent, and the sampled GPU utilization was 82–93%.
