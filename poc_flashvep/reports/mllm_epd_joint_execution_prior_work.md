# Prior work on grouped encoding and joint Encode–Prefill execution

This audit covers grouped CUDA-ready encoding (A), joint Encode–Prefill work on a Decode GPU (B), and architecture-specific execution (C). Public sources inspected are pinned below. A paper establishes that a mechanism has been proposed. A code link establishes the behavior of the cited path. Runtime parity with a paper or feasibility on the H200 configuration remains UNVERIFIED where indicated.

## Comparisons that isolate the proposed mechanism

**Direction A.** Freeze the grouped encoder across all four arms and compare paired Early/Late differences within each recipient. The effect specific to choosing D is the request-level difference (Late D − Early D) − (Late P − Early P). Keep whole-batch NativeStrong and grouped late arms in the comparison to account for lost encoder batching efficiency. Record device completion for each group, sufficient contiguous image rows and completed consumer work before the last actual Vision group. The previous single-request study saw early submission in 54/54 cases and early retirement of visual LM rows in 0/54 cases. Its shape-primed paired D increment was −51.80 ms. [Pinned follow-up](https://github.com/jyssys/MLLM-EP/blob/d76e9785e0928811051c9fd8dd32bf5c7497df5b/poc_flashvep/reports/mllm_epd_ready_prefix_recipient_followup.md)

**Direction B.** The clearest comparison retains the suffix on D and changes who encodes its images. Both arms transfer the same committed causal Prefix KV from P and run the same suffix LM work on D. In the joint arm D encodes only the suffix images assigned to it, keeps their embeddings local and yields to native Decode at the next legal completion boundary. E must measurably omit those images. A split-window arm can persist local embeddings while other requests decode, provided it charges their residency, queueing, acknowledgements and any admission delay. Encoder-only assistance isolates the effect of reducing E. Generic suffix assistance isolates the effect of reducing P. The earlier project saw 32.2% less Vision-path latency for a physical split, and separately measured native max ITL increasing from 6.99 ms to 66.20–123.26 ms in active-Decode interventions. It also found that hard image cuts can forfeit usable offload opportunities with slice-aware transport. [Earlier intervention](https://github.com/jyssys/MLLM-EP/blob/f12303c030130ac348ed3f89d4d21b886de088b6/poc_flashvep/reports/mllm_epd_d_side_encoder_sharing.md) · [Earlier slice-aware implementation](https://github.com/jyssys/MLLM-EP/blob/e5fdd1260179568da654ca2c83f37cd8f26c880e/poc_flashvep/reports/mllm_epd_h200_dynamic_slice_aware_final_summary.md)

**Communication discriminator.** The pinned Qwen2.5-VL-7B geometry yields 7 KiB of BF16 LM-ready embedding per visual row (3584 × 2) and 56 KiB of BF16 KV per prompt row across 28 layers (28 × 4 × 128 × 2 × 2). These are logical bytes. A D-produced prefix sent to P and later returned inside full Prompt KV adds 56 KiB of KV traffic per prefix row, compared with avoiding at most 7 KiB of E→P input per row. Keeping the prefix on D and returning only the missing suffix can bring logical KV traffic back to baseline, with bidirectional transfers and a more complex allocation and acknowledgement path. The D-local suffix avoids that reverse handoff and still requires the ready P prefix before LM attention. The estimates exclude page padding, raw media, metadata, host copy and synchronization. [Pinned model config](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct/raw/cc594898137f460bfe9f0759e9844b3ce807cfb5/config.json)

## Novelty decision

Grouped, actual CUDA-ready early Prefill has published and public implementation precedent. B's testable increment is the joint removal of source Vision work, source Prefill work and the matching suffix Embedding transfer while D retains state and protects native Decode. The audited sources cover the components and colocated joint Encode–Prefill. Whether the same request-local, bounded suffix mechanism is implemented in an unaudited system remains UNVERIFIED. A distinct contribution requires physical feasibility and a matched Decode-protected online gain over the generic suffix control. The previous 100–250 ms observed idle opportunities have diagnostic evidence status and guide physical screening.
