# Autonomous candidate discovery for fixed-role MLLM EPD

The target is original BF16 Qwen2.5-VL-7B on three H200s with the E/P/D roles preserved. The online gate is at least 15% lower TTFT p95 against an eligible strong baseline with Native TPOT p95 and ITL p99 degradation at most 5%. The primary workload is the frozen natural Very-long Fixed16 cohort. The disjoint Natural EOS cohort measures scope. Larger repeated primary censuses measure persistence with the same documents. [Frozen workload contract](mllm_epd_idle_execution_workloads_and_vision_geometry.md)

Earlier clean N24 same-budget whole-request D deflection improved TTFT p95 from 10.519 to 7.486 s and goodput from 0.384 to 0.852 req/s, while TPOT p95 and ITL p99 worsened by 41.4% and 102.0%. The earlier observer-failed GPU trace and the new held-out replay motivate bounded work. Candidate selection uses executable quanta and their full dependencies. ## Strong same-budget controls

1. **Native B8192/S128 1E/1P/1D.** Match the original BF16 checkpoint, APC-off contract, media, batch and graph settings, attention backend, EC/NIXL, KV reservation and paired open-loop arrival traces. Repeat independent restarts and retain all attempts.
2. **Deadline-aware generic D-local LM placement.** Compare no deflection, whole-request deflection with D-local chunked prefill, deadline-bounded arbitrary-token chunks and layer-boundary variants that the runtime can execute. Record current P work, active D context and real handoff state. The historical naive arm gives a whole-request physical and online comparator. A source-described or reimplemented Kairos-style policy is scoped to its own observed behavior.
3. **Generic physical vision sharing.** Compare fused E, local frontier, same-cut E, fixed remote fraction and whole-image D. Include same-device layer-yield or spatial sharing when runtime support is verified. Use matching hot weights and memory reservations so both the selected quantum and the architecture cut must earn an improvement.
4. **Same-frontier early P.** Feed P and P-or-D the identical captured causal frontiers, then consider arrival-matched ordinary LM prompt control. A ready-marker lead alone conveys scheduling opportunity and still needs a completed GPU operation and a request-level effect.
