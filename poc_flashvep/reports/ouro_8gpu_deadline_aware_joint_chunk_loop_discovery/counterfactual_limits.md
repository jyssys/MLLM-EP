# Counterfactual limits

Recorded future duration is a label, not an online feature.
The stock-policy next-token label includes the stock policy's future decisions.
Changing C/G changes those decisions and later queue states.
The same recorded residual cannot serve as each candidate's causal completion cost.

Logical issued depth is not necessarily physically completed depth.
The observer records pending-event count but not every event's readiness at the pre-selection boundary.
Its snapshot occurs after native batch selection.
That snapshot cannot certify every alternate action's resource allocation and KV publication.

Summing stream intervals can double-count waits shared across streams.
The candidate-specific simulator must distinguish ready work, queued work, service cost, and safe publication.
It must validate predictions against independent live timelines.

The offline cost envelope does not make those guarantees.
Its output is a mechanism diagnostic, not a qualified serving upper bound.
Qualified opportunity prevalence, online false-feasibility rate, and E2E speedup remain NOT_AVAILABLE.
